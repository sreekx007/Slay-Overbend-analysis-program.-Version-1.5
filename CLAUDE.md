# Working agreements

## Reply style — DEFAULT

Answer, then stop. Reasons on request only.

* Lead with the answer or the status. No preamble, no recap of the question.
* Prefer a table or a short list over prose.
* State a number, a status and a file path. Do not explain why unless asked.
* Do not volunteer the reasoning behind a result, the history of how it was
  found, or what it means for the next step.
* "Outstanding" / "next" is fine as a one-line list, not a discussion.
* If something is wrong or blocked, say what in one line. Detail on request.

This applies to chat replies. It does NOT apply to:

* **Commit messages** — these stay full. They are the durable record of why
  a change was made, and a terse commit loses what no one will reconstruct.
* **`docs/`** — the specs, `RESULTS.md`, `VALIDATION.md` and both YAML
  registers keep their current depth. They exist to be read cold, later.
* **Code comments** — match the surrounding density, which here is high and
  deliberate.

So: terse in conversation, complete in the repository.

## Corrections

If I correct a number, correct it in the file and say so in one line. Do
not re-explain the original mistake.
