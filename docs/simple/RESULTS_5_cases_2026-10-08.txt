The `Xb/GDTP` column printed above quotes the BODY's strain against the
GD-TP peak, and that compares two different pieces of steel: a GD-TP's
governing strain is at the PIPE-TO-COMPONENT JUNCTION, outboard of the
body, which is Xe's territory and not Xb's. `study_simple.py` now prints
Xe/GDTP instead. Recomputed from the figures above:

than a strain field; Xe and the moments are unaffected.
