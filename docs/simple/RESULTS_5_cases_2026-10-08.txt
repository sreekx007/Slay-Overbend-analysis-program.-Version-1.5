NOTE added after the run, 8 Oct 2026.
The `Xb/GDTP` column printed above quotes the BODY's strain against the
GD-TP peak, and that compares two different pieces of steel: a GD-TP's
governing strain is at the PIPE-TO-COMPONENT JUNCTION, outboard of the
body, which is Xe's territory and not Xb's. `study_simple.py` now prints
Xe/GDTP instead. Recomputed from the figures above:

MOMENT IS REPRODUCED WITHIN 1% ON ALL FIVE, which is EI matching doing what
it was set up to do and is the strongest result here. Adjacent-pipe strain
lands within 3.3% on four of five; B4 is +9.4% and is already the outlier
in the GD-TP set itself (the +13.0% moment overshoot, ledger Sec.7 item 6),
so the anomaly tracks the case rather than the method.

Xb is far below the GD-TP peak by construction, not by failure: a 917 GPa
elastic body barely bends and sheds its curvature outboard. A1 and B1 carry
TWO elements in Xb at this mesh, so their body strain reports a mesh rather
than a strain field; Xe and the moments are unaffected.
