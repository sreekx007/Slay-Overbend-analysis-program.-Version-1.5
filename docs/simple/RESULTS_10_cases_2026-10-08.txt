THE TWO COLUMNS BEHAVE DIFFERENTLY, and that is the most useful thing in
this table.

  MOMENT IS UNBIASED. Mean +0.05%, band -1.0 to +0.8%, six high and four
  low. That is scatter about zero -- the signature of a quantity being
  reproduced, with the residual being mesh and discretisation rather than a
  modelling difference.

  STRAIN IS BIASED LOW. Nine of ten fall in -1.0 to -3.3%, mean -2.15%, and
  every one of the nine is NEGATIVE. A band that tight with a consistent
  sign is a systematic offset, not scatter: one cause, not ten. B4 is the
  only case on the other side at +9.4%.

So the simplification reproduces the moment outright and the strain with a
roughly 2% shortfall that has a cause worth finding. Nothing here identifies
that cause, and the obvious candidate -- the stand-in keeping the pipeline's
extreme-fibre distance -- does NOT explain it, because the peak sits in
pipeline-section steel in both models, where the fibre distance is the same.

THE THREE SWEEPS THE SECOND FIVE WERE CHOSEN TO COMPLETE

  length, R = 70 / 65 mm :  2.5 D 96.7% | 10 D 99.0% | 20 D 97.3%
  length, R = 85 / 53 mm :  2.5 D 97.0% | 10 D 98.7% | 20 D 98.1% | 40 D 109.4%
  radius, 32 mm / 2.5 D  :  R70 98.1%   | R85 98.0%  | R100 97.6%

RADIUS DOES NOT MOVE THE AGREEMENT: 97.6 to 98.1% across 70, 85 and 100 m.
LENGTH DOES NOT MOVE IT UP TO 20 D, on either wall. The single departure is
B4 at 40 D, which is also the only case spanning nearly two roller bays and
the only GD-TP in Paper 1 carrying a moment overshoot (+13.0%, Sec. 8
item 6). Two independent length sweeps agreeing to 20 D and one breaking at
40 D isolates the departure to that case rather than to the method.

Xb/Xe falls as the modulus ratio rises -- 0.28 to 0.43 at 349 GPa, 0.10 to
0.15 at 682, 0.05 to 0.08 at 917 -- which is the stiff-inclusion mechanism
the control measures: a body that cannot yield sheds curvature outboard, and
sheds more of it the stiffer it is.
